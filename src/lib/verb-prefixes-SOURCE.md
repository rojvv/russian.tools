# Verb Prefix Explorer

`verb-prefix-data.ts` is an editorial selection of 43 cards in eight non-motion
verb families: говорить, читать, писать, делать, учить, смотреть, думать, работать.
Meanings, usage notes, and example sentences were written for this tool. The
catalogue is not an imported dictionary or an exhaustive list of derivatives.

## Editorial rules

- Store actual infinitives explicitly; never construct verbs by adding a prefix.
- Each card covers selected senses. Do not imply that a prefix always has one
  meaning, or that a verb has only the meanings shown.
- Separate lexical meaning from aspect. Договорить is paired with договаривать,
  not with говорить. Договориться has a separate reflexive meaning and pair.
- Use conventional unprefixed imperfectives where appropriate, such as
  писать / написать and учить / научить. Base cards describe the activity
  independently; their empty perfective field does not assert that no partner exists.
- Leave imperfective fields empty for bounded activities such as поговорить,
  почитать, поделать, and поработать. Do not invent exact partners.
- Include selected reflexive derivatives when the contrast is useful:
  договориться versus договорить, научиться versus научить.
- Prefix labels describe the selected derivative. They are not automatic
  morphological segmentation of every form on the card.
- Search uses the displayed infinitives and bilingual meanings. Inflected forms
  are handled by the separate conjugator. All cards in a matching family remain
  visible to preserve the comparison; matching cards are marked.

## Reference checks

The dictionaries hosted by Gramota.ru were consulted for selected lexical and
aspect distinctions. The page links to a dictionary lookup for each card.

- [Договорить, Большой толковый словарь русского языка](https://gramota.ru/poisk?dicts%5B%5D=42&mode=slovari&query=договорить):
  finishing an utterance; imperfective договаривать.
- [Договаривать, Большой толковый словарь русских глаголов](https://gramota.ru/poisk?dicts%5B0%5D=52&mode=slovari&query=договаривать&simple=0):
  finishing speech and the separate reflexive agreement sense.
- [Заговорить](https://gramota.ru/poisk?mode=slovari&query=заговорить):
  beginning to speak and the заговаривать form.
- [Учить, Большой универсальный словарь](https://gramota.ru/poisk?mode=slovari&page=3&query=учит&simple=0):
  teaching and learning senses with научить and выучить.
- [Осмотреть, Большой универсальный словарь](https://gramota.ru/poisk?mode=slovari&page=2&query=усмотреть&simple=0):
  осматривать / осмотреть and the о- prefix.
- [Думать](https://gramota.ru/poisk?mode=all&query=думать): thinking senses and
  the relationship with подумать.

Tests check every displayed infinitive and its aspect against the project's
existing OpenRussian verb dictionary. This checks conjugation link coverage;
it does not independently validate every example or semantic distinction.
